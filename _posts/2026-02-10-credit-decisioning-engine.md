## What this is
An end-to-end underwriting and pricing engine built on LendingClub-style data.

## Why it matters
Demonstrates how a modern lender separates:
- approval
- pricing
- limit assignment  
while balancing growth vs risk-adjusted returns.

## What I built
- PD model (origination features only)
- Policy rules + decline reason codes
- Risk-based APR tiering
- Approved amount recommendation
- Backtesting
